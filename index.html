Lily Pond

An interactive Monet-inspired koi, frog & turtle sanctuary

Pond Details & How to Interact ▾

Interactive Fluid Ripples

Glide your mouse or touch the water surface to create physical water ripples. Splashes refract underwater lighting and gently nudge resting lily pads.

Living Pond Ecology

Watch Japanese Nishikigoi (koi) cruise gracefully beneath the water, frogs leap across buoyant lotus pads, and a turtle occasionally surface for air.

Nourish & Feed

Click the feed button to scatter food pellets across the surface. Koi dart and gather eagerly to nibble.

Diurnal Natural Light

Experience realistic ambient shifts from Dawn and Day to Dusk and Dark, rendered through custom WebGL shaders inspired by Claude Monet's water garden.
